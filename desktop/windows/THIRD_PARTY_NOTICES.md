## FFmpeg (Windows 0.2)

Separate, shared-LGPL FFmpeg executable/DLL distribution from BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds:
https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds/releases/tag/autobuild-2026-11-01-12-49
`ffmpeg-n8.1.3-29-gad3b795b72-win64-lgpl-shared-8.1.zip`
SHA256: `786b43f77ea260af71fbb1bcdaabd43df83492122fb5575027f659d490d30b37`.
All upstream non-binary distribution contents, including licenses/notices/build
information, are retained in tools/ffmpeg-distribution. Build scripts and source
references: https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds/tree/autobuild-2026-11-01-12-49
and https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/tree/ad3b795b72 . No proprietary static link
or modification of FFmpeg is made. FFmpeg is launched as a separate process.
For intentional tool replacement/rebuilding, regenerate SHA256-FFMPEG.json over
all replacement executable/DLL/WAV files; the manifest is an integrity check,
not a prohibition on modifying/replacing LGPL components. Channel test WAVs are
synthetically generated by this repository's build recipe; no third-party audio.
